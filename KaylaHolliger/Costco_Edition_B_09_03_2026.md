## What the historical filings say

Costco’s reported net sales rose from $237.7 billion in FY2023 to $249.6 billion in FY2024 and $269.9 billion in FY2025. The respective total-company comparable-sales changes were 3%, 5%, and 6%; FY2023 had 53 weeks, while FY2024 and FY2025 had 52. [[FY2023 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm) [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Membership-fee revenue increased from $4.580 billion in FY2023 to $4.828 billion in FY2024 and $5.323 billion in FY2025. At FY2025 year-end, renewal rates were 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide; the company also says FY2025 fee growth reflected both new sign-ups and the September 2024 U.S./Canada fee increase. These results support the view that membership fees are a recurring, but not entirely volume-only, earnings driver. [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

Net income grew from $6.292 billion ($14.16 diluted EPS) in FY2023 to $7.367 billion ($16.56) in FY2024 and $8.099 billion ($18.21) in FY2025. Operating cash flow likewise increased from $11.068 billion to $11.339 billion to $13.335 billion. This is a constructive multi-year profitability and cash-generation trend, though it does not by itself establish intrinsic value. [[FY2023 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983223000042/cost-20230903.htm) [[FY2024 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983224000049/cost-20240901.htm) [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

The warehouse footprint rose from 861 at the end of FY2023 to 890 at the end of FY2024 and 914 at the end of FY2025. New locations can expand sales, but Costco cautions that expansion can bring lower initial profitability and cannibalization in existing markets. [[FY2024 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983224000049/cost-20240901.htm) [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

## Recent operating evidence and management commentary

Costco’s fiscal-Q3 2026 release reported 11.6% year-over-year sales growth to $69.15 billion, 9.8% reported comparable-sales growth, and 6.6% adjusted comparable-sales growth excluding gasoline-price and foreign-exchange effects. For the first 36 weeks, adjusted comparable sales rose 6.6% and digitally enabled comparable sales rose 21.1%; Q3 net income was $2.19 billion, or $4.93 per diluted share. [[Q3 2026 results]](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

The May 2026 sales release extended that trend: for the first 39 weeks, net sales were up 10.0% and total-company comparable sales were up 6.7% excluding gasoline-price and foreign-exchange effects. Costco reported 931 warehouses at May 31, 2026. [[May 2026 sales release]](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-May-Sales-Results/default.aspx)

On the Q3 earnings call, CEO Ron Vachris said that low-price positioning remained the company’s focus amid macro uncertainty, described recent price reductions as strategic, and said technology investments were producing productivity gains. Management also reduced its fiscal-2026 net-new-opening outlook to 26 from prior guidance, with delayed openings moving into FY2027. These are management statements, not independently verified forecasts. [[Q3 2026 call transcript]](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/28/costco-cost-q3-2026-earnings-transcript/)

## Valuation check and key risks

The available price snapshot is $926.78 per COST share on September 3, 2026. Dividing that price by FY2025 diluted EPS of $18.21 gives an approximate **50.9× trailing FY2025 P/E**. This is a simple calculation, not a DCF or a forward P/E; it shows that the market price embeds a substantial premium to the most recently audited annual earnings base. [[Price snapshot]](https://ycharts.com/companies/COST/price) [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm)

The upside case is continued high renewal, healthy comparable-sales growth, successful international expansion, and productivity gains. The downside case is that a slowdown in comparable sales, renewal rates, or margins would matter disproportionately when the starting earnings multiple is high. Gasoline prices and foreign exchange also affect reported sales and margin percentages, which is why the adjusted comparable-sales measures above are more useful for judging current underlying demand. [[FY2025 10-K]](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm) [[Q3 2026 results]](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx)

## Current call

**Watch / defer (wait for more information).** Costco’s historical and recent operating evidence is strong, but this report does not yet demonstrate enough intrinsic-value upside to offset a roughly 51× trailing earnings starting point. I would change this call to **initiate** if a conservative DCF and peer-multiple analysis showed a meaningful margin of safety at the market price while adjusted comparable sales, renewal rates, and operating margins remained durable.
